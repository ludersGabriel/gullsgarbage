The jam kicked off with the theme drop at 6pm. We spent the first hour arguing about whether a gull is a bird or a menace (verdict: both), then scoped the smallest thing that could still feel fast.

## The loop

- three laps
- one dive
- a podium no one will see

```text
countdown -> race -> podium
```

The camera swings on a spring. It felt wrong for exactly four hours, then it clicked.
